# Kintsugi Care Dashboard

KINTSUGI CARE — FARMER DASHBOARD REDESIGN & FEATURE UPGRADE

You are an expert product designer, UX designer, frontend engineer, and mobile-app designer.

I already have an existing farmer dashboard for a livestock health platform called Kintsugi Care. Do NOT completely replace the existing product structure. Upgrade and redesign it while preserving the simple, familiar navigation and farmer-friendly workflow.

The target users are rural livestock farmers, including users with limited digital literacy, low-end smartphones, limited internet connectivity, and preference for local languages.

The final result should feel like a real, production-ready healthcare/agriculture technology product, not a college project, generic dashboard, or template.

1. PRODUCT PURPOSE

Kintsugi Care is a livestock health platform that helps farmers:

Monitor their animals

Report health problems

Get an urgency/risk assessment

Receive immediate general safety/first-aid guidance while waiting for a veterinarian

Connect with veterinarians

Track veterinary cases

Monitor recovery

Track vaccinations

Receive local health alerts

Understand possible exposure of other animals/locations

Continue reporting even without internet

Receive preventive health guidance

IMPORTANT:

Kintsugi Care must NOT present itself as replacing a veterinarian.

The system can perform risk/priority assessment, but it must not claim to definitively diagnose a disease.

Veterinarians provide disease-specific diagnosis, treatment and professional instructions.
